About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in the Animal–Environment Interactions Lab at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior and Universität Konstanz, Germany. My work focuses on movement and spatial ecology, wildlife management, and species conservation, with a particular interest in birds and raptors.

Before starting my PhD, I served as a Lecturer at Federal University Gashua, Nigeria, where I taught over 20 undergraduate courses, supervised research projects, and contributed to departmental leadership. I am also an active wildlife consultant, citizen scientist, and conservation advocate.

Research

My research explores how animals move through changing environments, with a focus on raptors and dryland ecosystems. I work extensively with GPS tracking data, spatial modeling, and ecological monitoring.

Projects
