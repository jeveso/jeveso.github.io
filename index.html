About Me

I work at the intersection of movement ecology, conservation science, and science coordination. As a PhD Candidate in the Animal–Environment Interactions Lab at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior and Universität Konstanz, I use high-resolution tracking data and spatial modeling to study how vultures and raptors navigate changing dryland landscapes.

Before moving to Germany, I spent years as a Lecturer at Federal University Gashua in northeastern Nigeria, where I taught wildlife ecology, supervised student research, led a department, and built the region's first birdwatching community from the ground up. That dual experience — doing the science and organizing the people around it — is the thread through everything I do.

I'm driven by the belief that conservation works when researchers, communities, and institutions coordinate effectively. Whether I'm analyzing vulture movement data in R, building a citizen science network in Yobe State, or coordinating field logistics across institutions, the goal is the same: turning ecological knowledge into lasting impact.

Research

My research asks how vultures and raptors respond to global change across Africa's dryland ecosystems. Using high-resolution GPS tracking data and spatial models, I examine how land-use change, climate shifts, and human activity reshape animal movement, space use, and survival — with direct implications for conservation planning in the Sahel and beyond.

At MPI-AB, I work within the IMPRS-QBEE program, contributing to collaborative projects on vulture movement ecology at continental scales, developing reproducible analytical workflows, and presenting findings at international symposia.

Projects

Vulture Movement & Global Change

Spatio-temporal analysis of vulture behavior and space use in response to land-use change and climate variability across dryland Africa. Presented at IMPRS-QBEE annual retreats (Freiburg, 2023) and selection symposia (Konstanz, 2024). Uses GPS tracking data from multiple vulture species to model how environmental change alters ranging behavior.

GPS tracking spatial ecology R MPI-AB

Drylands Project (MPI-AB)

Continental-scale analysis of vulture movement ecology using the Movebank global dataset. Responsibilities include meta-information stratification, ecological modeling, data quality assessment, and building reproducible analytical pipelines for a collaborative, multi-researcher team.

Movebank big data reproducible research

Avifauna Diversity Studies — Nigeria

Multi-year field studies documenting bird diversity, abundance, and ecological distribution across savanna and wetland ecosystems in northeastern Nigeria. Resulted in multiple peer-reviewed publications and contributed baseline data to the Nigeria Bird Atlas Project.

field ecology biodiversity Nigeria

Wildlife Consultancy & Zoo Management

Applied conservation work including enclosure design, animal enrichment programs, taxidermy, park landscaping, and conservation education programming across multiple wildlife facilities including Ogba Zoological Garden and Nature Park.

conservation education zoo management consultancy

Leadership & Science Coordination

For a deeper look at my coordination philosophy and experience → Coordination & Management page.

🦅

Northeast Coordinator — Wildlife Society of Nigeria (WISON)

Served as regional coordinator for WISON's northeastern chapter, organizing members across multiple states, representing the region at national conferences, and facilitating collaboration between university researchers, wildlife agencies, and local conservation practitioners.

🔭

Founder & Coordinator — Gashua Bird Club

Built northeastern Nigeria's first birdwatching community from scratch: recruited members, organized regular field outings, developed educational programming, and led community sensitization on bird conservation in Yobe State. The club contributed data to the Nigeria Bird Atlas Project and introduced citizen science practice to an underserved region.

Acting Head of Department — Federal University Gashua

Served multiple terms leading the department, overseeing academic programming, coordinating teaching staff, managing curriculum review processes, and representing the department in faculty and university-level governance.

🗺️

Citizen Scientist — Nigeria Bird Atlas Project (NiBAP)

Active contributor to the national bird distribution mapping effort, completing 25 pentad surveys — generating baseline distribution data used by researchers and conservation planners across West Africa.
