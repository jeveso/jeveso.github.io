Science Coordination & Management

Building the teams, networks, and institutions that connect ecological research to conservation outcomes.

Approach

Conservation works when researchers, communities, and institutions coordinate effectively. My career has always combined doing the science with organizing the people around it — leading departments, building citizen science networks, managing grants, and bridging research institutions across continents.

I bring experience from two very different institutional environments: a growing Nigerian university where I built programs from the ground up, and one of Europe's leading research institutes where I contribute to large-scale collaborative projects. That range has taught me how to work across resource levels, cultures, and organizational structures to get science done.
