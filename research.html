Research

Research Overview and Interests

My research focuses on understanding how animals move through dynamic and changing environments, with a particular emphasis on raptors and dryland ecosystems. I work at the intersection of movement ecology, spatial ecology, wildlife conservation, and environmental change.

As a PhD Candidate in the Animal–Environment Interactions Lab at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior and Universität Konstanz, I analyze high-resolution movement datasets, develop spatial models, and explore how global change influences species behavior and survival.

Research Interests

  • Movement & Spatial Ecology
  • Wildlife Management
  • Species Conservation
  • Avifauna Diversity
  • Raptors and Dryland Ecosystems
