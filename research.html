Research

Research Interests

My research focuses on movement and spatial ecology, wildlife management, and species conservation, with a particular emphasis on birds—especially raptors. I am interested in how animals navigate dynamic and changing environments, how global change influences their behavior and survival, and how ecological data can inform conservation strategies.

Technical Skills
  • Data Analysis: R (sf, move, move2, terra, dismo, sdm, biomod2, tidyverse, ggplot2), PAST, SPSS
  • Scientific Writing: Microsoft Office, Mendeley, Zotero, Overleaf
  • Reproducibility: GitHub version control
  • Field Ecology: Bird surveys, ecological monitoring, wildlife handling
