Research & Publications

Movement ecology, vulture conservation, and spatial ecology across Africa's dryland ecosystems.

Research Interests

My research focuses on movement and spatial ecology, wildlife management, and species conservation, with a particular emphasis on raptors in dryland Africa. I study how animals navigate dynamic and changing environments, how global change — including land-use change, climate variability, and human activity — influences their behavior and survival, and how ecological data can inform conservation strategies at landscape and continental scales.

Technical Skills

  • Data Analysis & Spatial Modeling: R (sf, move, move2, terra, dismo, sdm, biomod2, tidyverse, ggplot2), PAST, SPSS
  • Scientific Writing & Reference Management: Overleaf (LaTeX), Microsoft Office, Mendeley, Zotero
  • Reproducibility & Version Control: Git, GitHub
  • Field Methods: Bird surveys (point counts, transects), ecological monitoring, wildlife handling, raptor identification
